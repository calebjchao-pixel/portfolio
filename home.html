Job

Give every remaining page one obvious door.

Holds

  • One tall photo card per page
  • Page name, one line of what’s inside
  • Footer underneath: contact, links, copyright

Rules

  • Four cards, one per page — no duplicates of section 2
  • Contact details repeat here as plain text

Decisions

  1. About sits after the work. Visitors are deciding whether you can shoot what they need; the photos answer that fastest. Flip the two only if the site is mainly for crew-lead hiring.
  2. The hero carries no nav. The menu bar appears once the hero is scrolled past, so the first screen is only the logo.
  3. Five projects, four doors. Section 2 shows work, section 4 routes to pages. They must not become two versions of the same grid.

Open

  2. Does the gallery stay a separate page, or fold into projects?
  3. Is there a services or rates section, and would it go between 3 and 4?
